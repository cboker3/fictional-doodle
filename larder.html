Install Instructions

Follow these steps to install Larder App on an Android device.

1

Download the APK

Use the download button above from your Android device's browser.

2

Allow installs from this source

When prompted, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager in Android Settings.

3

Open and install

Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder and tap Install.

4

Launch Larder App

Find Larder App on your home screen or app drawer and open it up.
